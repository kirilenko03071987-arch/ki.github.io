Belarus is a presidential republic located in Eastern Europe. The country has no access to the sea, but its territory is rich in rivers, lakes and marshes. Belarus borders Russia, Ukraine, Poland, Lithuania and Latvia. The capital is the city of Minsk. The official languages are Belarusian and Russian. The national currency is the Belarusian ruble.

State Symbols

Flag, emblem and anthem — symbols of the nation's sovereignty

🇧🇾

Flag

The red color symbolizes freedom and the memory of a heroic past, while green stands for hope, spring and rebirth. The white ornament on the left is a symbol of ancient Belarusian culture and hard work.

🌿

Emblem

The green outline of the country against a background of golden sunrays above the globe. It symbolizes sovereignty, territorial unity and peaceful skies over Belarus.

🎵

Anthem

"We, the Belarusians" is the national anthem, glorifying the people, their unity and love for their native land. Its words and music reflect the country's historical journey.

Cities

Every city holds its own unique story

Minsk

The capital of Belarus and its largest cultural and economic center. A city with more than 950 years of history. Main attractions: the National Library (one of the largest in the world), the Upper Town, the Trinity Suburb, the Holy Spirit Cathedral and the City Hall.

Brest

A hero city in western Belarus. The legendary Brest Fortress was the first to face the attack in June 1941. The Kholm Gate is the main symbol of the fortress. It is also home to one of the first street lanterns in Europe.

Grodno

One of the oldest cities in the country, known as the "western gate of Belarus". Its main attraction is the Old Castle (11th–19th centuries) and the New Castle. It is also famous for the Kalozha Church (12th century) and the Great Choral Synagogue.

Vitebsk

The birthplace of Marc Chagall and the home of the famous "Slavianski Bazaar" festival. Main attractions: the Holy Resurrection Church and the City Hall on Resurrection Square, as well as the Assumption Cathedral.

National Cuisine

Draniki

Potato pancakes — the calling card of Belarusian cuisine. Made from grated potato and fried in a pan.

Borsch

A rich beetroot soup. The Belarusian version is often cooked without cabbage, using a smoked meat broth.

Kolduny

Potato dumplings filled with minced meat. Served hot, often with sour cream.

Fun Facts

A few surprising things about Belarus

1

Belarus is home to the largest ancient forest in Europe — Belovezhskaya Pushcha — as well as the world's largest population of European bison.

2

The country has two centers of Europe: one geographic, one geodetic. Both are marked with commemorative signs.

3

The National Library in Minsk, opened in 2006, is one of the largest in the world.
